7. KEY BUSINESS INSIGHTS
- The dataset contains an imbalanced attrition target.
- Employees who left generally show lower median income and shorter company tenure.
- Overtime is associated with a higher attrition rate.
- Sales Representatives show a relatively high attrition rate.
- Single employees show a higher attrition rate than married employees.
- Several experience and income-related features are strongly correlated.

8. MODELING CONSIDERATIONS
- Use stratified train-test splitting because the target classes are imbalanced.
- Evaluate precision, recall, F1-score and ROC-AUC along with accuracy.
- Fit preprocessing and resampling operations using training data only.
- Investigate correlated features during model comparison and interpretation.
- Treat EDA findings as associations, not causal relationships.
